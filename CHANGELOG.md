# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-10-03

First release: 0.5 after use. RAW16 from RAWfish is tested on real files;
RAW8, RAW10 and RAW12 only on synthetic ones.

- The fiat family list in About now includes fiat ratio, links fiat lux, and
  has corrected links for fiat glossa and fiat cor.

## 0.5 — 2026-09-29

Pre-release.

- **Looks.** Ten built in: none, recover, warm, golden, cool, punchy, matte,
  mono, sepia and facet, applied with a strength from 0 to 150 %. Save your
  own with Save look in the pull-down menu; hold one to delete it. A look
  keeps light, colour, detail and effects, never the crop.
- **Colour mixer.** Hue, saturation and lightness for eight colour bands, in
  colour › mixer. Taking colour away keeps the lightness, so a blue sky turns
  grey, not pale.
- **Toning.** A colour for the shadows and one for the highlights, with a
  balance, in effects › toning. Sepia and duotone.
- **Pattern.** The triangles Sailfish draws on its own backgrounds, measured
  from the Settings app, in effects › pattern.
- **Camera colour.** RAW files are now developed with the colour transform the
  camera recorded for each shot, and match RAWfish's own JPEG.
- **Memory cards.** Photos are also found on a memory card, in Pictures,
  Pictures/Camera, Pictures/RAWfish and DCIM.
- **Orientation.** An orientation written in the sidecar is used before
  matching the JPEG, ready for when RAWfish records it.
- Temperature and tint are about twice as strong as in 0.2. Photos edited in
  0.2 with these sliders will look stronger.
- A new icon.

## 0.2 — 2026-09-27

Pre-release.

- Blown highlights shown in red, in the preview only.
- Perspective correction, vertical and horizontal.
- Straighten up to ±45°.
- A 100 % loupe in the detail tool.
- Reads RAW8, RAW10 and RAW12 as well as RAW16. The three are tested on
  synthetic files only.
- Looks for RAWfish files in Pictures/RAWfish.

## 0.1 — 2026-09-27

First pre-release: develops RAWfish RAW files and JPEG photos. Light, colour,
crop, detail and effects with a live preview; every export is a new JPEG in
Pictures/fiat imago.
